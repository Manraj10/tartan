# Code of conduct

Tartan follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

If someone's behaviour in an issue, pull request or discussion makes you uncomfortable, contact the
maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** works for
conduct reports too) or through [GitHub](https://github.com/Manraj10). Reports are kept confidential.
